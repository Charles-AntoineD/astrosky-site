How to joinFAQ

Arrive in the Constellation

The spawn is called the Constellation: seven floating islands linked by bridges, like stars joined into a constellation, under an eight-point pole star.

At its edge floats a monument: a dog astronaut swimming in zero gravity, looking toward the pole star.

What you do on AstroSky

One block, then a whole island

Start on a single magic block floating among the stars. Break it, and it comes back as something new: wood, ores, animals, and new phases as you go. Invite friends to your island and build it together.

An economy run by players

Sell to the server, run chest shops on your island, list items at the auction house and share an island bank with your team.

Goals for every session

Challenges guide your first hours, then give longer goals. The island level ranking measures what you build.

A store for looks only

The store sells cosmetics and donations only, such as titles and name colours. Nothing sold changes how the game plays, coins are never sold, and no purchase is random.

Update 1 is planned: jobs and bigger island teams.
