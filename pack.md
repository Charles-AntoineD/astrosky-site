# AstroSky — The AstroSky resource pack

**Optional.** AstroSky works with the default Minecraft textures. The server offers you the AstroSky pack when you
join: answer **No** and you play normally, answer **Yes** and Minecraft downloads it (about half a megabyte). Type
`/pack` in game to be offered it again later.

The AstroSky pack is free for everyone and never tied to a rank or a purchase.

[Version française](/fr/pack/)

## What is in the pack

It keeps the look of the game and polishes the night sky: twinkling stars, a finer glow around the sun and moon, clean
glass. No mod needed, light enough for any computer. It is the Vanilla Tweaks "Constellation" selection (11 packs),
with AstroSky additions: the phase bar in AstroSky colours, the pack icon and the title-screen lines.

## Download it yourself

Direct link: [astrosky-pack.zip](/pack/astrosky-pack.zip) (`https://astrosky.gg/pack/astrosky-pack.zip`). Put the zip
file, unopened, in your `resourcepacks` folder, then turn it on in Options → Resource Packs.

## Other options (optional)

You can also build the same selection yourself, or try other packs. We don't host these other packs, and each link
goes to the pack's official page.

### The same selection, from Vanilla Tweaks

1. Open the official picker: [vanillatweaks.net/picker/resource-packs](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/resource-packs/).
2. Choose the version of Minecraft you play with.
3. Tick these 11 packs:
   - **Terrain**: Twinkling Stars, HD Sun & Moon Glow, Circular Sun & Moon, Clearer Water, Bushy Leaves;
   - **Unobtrusive**: Clean Glass, Clean Stained Glass, Unobtrusive Rain, Lower Fire, Lower Shield;
   - **Aesthetic**: Colorful Enchanting Table Particles.
4. Leave **Warm Glow** unticked: it changes the colours of the light on AstroSky.
5. Click **Download**, put the zip file in your `resourcepacks` folder, then turn it on in Options → Resource Packs.

Twinkling Stars does not work with shader packs.

### Sharper textures: Faithful 32x

The game's textures redrawn at twice the resolution, in the same style. A little heavier than the default textures.
Official page: [faithfulpack.net](https://faithfulpack.net/), also on
[Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/faithful-32x).

### For powerful computers: Complementary shaders

Light, sky and reflections. They need the Iris mod and a strong graphics card, and they change the lighting of the
whole game. Official pages: [Complementary Reimagined](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-reimagined) and
[Iris](https://www.irisshaders.dev/).

These other packs are made by their own authors, not by AstroSky. Read their pages before you download anything. A
question about a pack? Write to us from the [contact page](/contact/).
