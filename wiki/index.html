How the magic block works

  • Each time you break the magic block, the server draws what comes next from one pool: blocks, animals and monsters together. The percentages below are the chance per break.
  • When an animal or a monster comes out, the block stays where it is and the mob appears on top of it. That break does not count toward your block total.
  • When a chest comes out, a second draw picks its rarity: Common 62%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 9%, Epic 4%. If the phase has no chest of that rarity, you get a Common one.
  • A phase lasts a set number of blocks. Some blocks are placed on purpose at the start of a phase; the phase pages list them.

Jobs

Pick up to 2 jobs in /skills. Each level gives you health, toughness or strength, titles at some levels, and coins for the actions of the job.

Jobs and level rewards

Island level

108 kinds of blocks give island level points, such as wood, glass, bricks and ore blocks, each up to a limit. Every other block gives 0. The highest island level you can reach by building is 382.
