Island level

What each block is worth, up to which limit, and how high your island can go.

Computed from the server files: Minecraft: Java Edition 26.2, AOneBlock 1.28.0, AuraSkills 2.4.0, Level 2.29.0 · data fingerprint 21288cd44a45

Points to level

Your island level is your points divided by 100, rounded down.

Highest level

If your island had every block below up to its limit, it would have 38,232 points: island level 382. That is the highest level you can reach by building.

How it is counted

  • Each block of your island gives its value in points. A block with a limit stops counting once your island has more than its limit: the server counts the limit plus one of that block, then nothing more.
  • Every other block is worth 0: 1,087 of the 1,195 blocks of the game, for example Dirt, Stone, Cobblestone, Grass Block, Sand.
  • Not counted: your Nether island, your End island, what is inside chests and other containers.
  • Mob spawners are worth 0 too.
  • Your starting island is counted when it is created and taken off: a new island starts at level 0.
  • Dying does not lower your island level.
  • The level is counted when you type /is level, at most once every 60 seconds.

Points max = (limit + 1) × value: what one kind of block can give at most. Blocks with the same value and limit share a row.

In game

  • /is level counts your island and gives its level.
  • /is value opens the menu of block values. /is value hand gives the value of the block in your hand, how many your island had at the last count, and its limit.
  • /is detail opens the list of the blocks counted on your island.
  • /is info lists your blocks at their limit, as counted at your last /is level, for example Maxed blocks (more of them add no island level): Beacon 4/4. A block shows there from its limit on; the server still counts one more, which is not worth building for.
  • /is top shows the islands with the highest level.

Block names are in English, as in the server files.
