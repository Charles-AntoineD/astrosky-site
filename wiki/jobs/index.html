Jobs and level rewards

Pick up to 2 jobs in /skills. Each pick, the first included, starts a 24-hour wait before the next. Jobs pay coins for the actions listed below, and each level adds to your stats.

Computed from the server files: Minecraft: Java Edition 26.2, AOneBlock 1.28.0, AuraSkills 2.4.0 · data fingerprint efcc74dfc2bb

What the stats do

  • Max HP: each point adds 1 max HP, which is half a heart. Up to 13 hearts (26 HP) every heart shows; above that the health bar is compressed, and the table below gives how many hearts you see.
  • Toughness: damage you take is reduced by 1 − 1.01−0.3 × Toughness.
  • Strength: each point adds 0.40% to your attack damage.
  • Regeneration: each point adds 0.02 HP to each natural healing tick.

Abilities

  • Stamina: +X Toughness while you hold a pickaxe. X grows from level 4, every 5 levels (level 4: 1; level 25: 5; level 50: 10; level 100: 20).
