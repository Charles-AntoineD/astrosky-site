# AstroSky — Privacy Policy

**Version:** `2026-10-23`

**Status:** AstroSky is not open yet: the server is closed to players (whitelist) and the store has not opened. This page describes how it will work from opening day; the version date changes if anything differs.

This policy covers the AstroSky Minecraft server (`astrosky.gg`), its store (`astrosky.gg/store`), its website
(`astrosky.gg`) and its contact address (`support@astrosky.gg`).

## The short version

- AstroSky is a Minecraft server for adults, 18 and over. It is run by Charles-Antoine Daneau in Québec, Canada, not by
  Mojang or Microsoft. We are responsible for protecting the personal information the server handles.
- To protect players, the server records what happens on it: public chat, private messages, the commands you type,
  blocks placed or broken, chest use, and when you join and leave. These records are **deleted automatically after
  90 days at most**. We keep only the last IP address you joined from, for safety, and delete it after 88
  days.
- Your game data is kept as long as your account exists; a sanction is kept for 12 months after it ends; an excerpt
  kept for a case follows that case's duration.
- **Nobody reads them unless there is a report, an incident or a written detection rule**, and our moderation tools
  log the reason before each look. Private messages are read only if one of the people in the conversation reports
  them, if an incident or a written rule concerns one of them, or if someone may be in danger.
- No person watches chat or private messages live, and no automated tool sanctions anyone. Two automatic checks can stop a public chat message before it is sent: a speed limit, which does not read it, and a filter for links to other websites and for a list of words, which reads the text to decide. We never sell your data and never use it for
  advertising, marketing or profiling.
- The server and its records are hosted by OVHcloud in Vint Hill, Virginia, United States (section 9).
- If you buy something, Tebex sells it to you and handles the payment. We never see your card number.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, for free: write to `support@astrosky.gg`.
- A parent or guardian can write to us to have a player's access removed and their data deleted; we never
  send them the player's data (section 10).

responsible for AstroSky and for the personal information the server, the website and our mailbox handle. Tebex is
responsible for the information you give it when you pay (section 8).

**Person in charge of the protection of personal information:** the operator of AstroSky, reachable at
`support@astrosky.gg` (subject: "Privacy"). We answer in English or French.

## 2. Who this policy is for

- people who play on the server;
- people who buy in our store;
- people who write to us;
- people who visit our website.

It does not cover services run by others, which have their own policies: your Minecraft and Microsoft account
(Microsoft), what you give Tebex at checkout (Tebex), Discord (except the alert copies in section 3), and social networks.

AstroSky is for people aged **18 and over**. We do not ask for your age and do not check identity documents. See
section 10 if a player turns out to be under 18.

Private messages between players (`/msg`, `/tell`) are turned off for now; if we turn them on, everything this
policy says about them applies, and we will announce it first.

The safety records are **not** in the 30-day backups. They have their own encrypted copy, made right after each
daily deletion and destroyed automatically 2 days after it is made, so no copy is ever older than 90 days.

The operator plays and moderates with an ordinary Minecraft game client. That client keeps, on the operator's own
computer, the chat and private messages it receives; these local logs are deleted after 90 days at most. Safety records are looked up from the
server console, never through the game chat.

**What we do not collect:** your age, your real name (unless you write it to us), identity documents, card numbers,
or voice. We do not use your IP address to find where you are.

## 4. How we use the safety records

- They are read **only** for a report, an incident or a written detection rule, and only from the server console,
  never from inside the game. Our moderation tools add an entry to an access log before each look (who, what, when,
  why); they can only add to it. The operator, who runs the machine, could open the files directly; our rules allow this only to repair the server. The AI assistant (section 13) is not allowed to open them either, and its administrator rights do not cover them. It can start backups and restore tests, which handle data by machine without showing it.
- A private message is read **only** if one of the people in the conversation reports it, if an incident or a written
  detection rule concerns one of them, or if someone may be in danger.
- **No person watches** chat or private messages live, and no role on the server can spy on private messages. Two automatic checks apply to public chat, not to private messages, and tell you when they stop a message. A speed limit stops messages sent too fast (more than 5 in 10 seconds); it never reads what they say. A filter reads the text of each public message as it is sent, in the server's memory, only to look for a link to another website or a word on our list (section 6); it looks at nothing else.
- A message stopped by the speed limit or the filter is not sent and is not kept in the safety records. The filter keeps no count at all; the speed limit's count lives in memory only and is dropped when you leave.
- They are never used for analytics, marketing, monetization or profiling.
- An excerpt needed for a case is kept with the case and follows its duration (section 3).
- If the automatic deletion and its retry within the hour both fail, the server **closes to players** until a
  deletion succeeds. Expired records are deleted no later than 24 hours after the first alert, and the failure is
  recorded as an incident.

## 5. Anti-cheat and linked accounts

- The anti-cheat looks at how accounts move and act in the game (for example speed, reach or impossible actions) to
  detect cheating. It works only inside the game, it only alerts us, and its results are used only against cheating.
- We keep **no history of IP addresses** and never sanction an IP address. A shared connection is never proof on its
  own. We check whether another account is being used to get around a sanction only after a report or in an open
  incident.

## 6. Automated decisions

**No automated tool sanctions you**: every sanction is decided by a person. The anti-cheat only alerts us. If you
send public chat messages too fast, the extra messages are not sent and you see a notice; this is not a sanction. The chat filter works the same way: a public message with a link to another website (our own site and our store excepted) or a word on our list (hate speech and slurs, explicit sexual words and sexual insults, and incitement directed at someone to harm themselves) is not sent, and you see a notice that does not say which word or link.
The server can also disconnect you automatically after 30 minutes idle, or if your client floods the chat or sends
too much data; you can rejoin right away. If we ever add an automated measure against an account (a stopped message is not one), it will be short (1 hour at most, or removal from the current session), limited to 3 per account
in 24 hours, with a person reviewing beyond that, and we will update this policy before it applies.

You can ask us what information an automated measure used, why it was applied and which factors mattered, ask for that
information to be corrected, and give your side to a person who can review the measure: write to `support@astrosky.gg`. This includes a message stopped by the speed limit or the filter: we can explain the rule and check your case from what you tell us.

## 7. What other players can see

Other players can see your username and skin, what you write in public chat, your private messages (only the person
you send them to), your island when they are a member or visitor, your island's level and ranking, your listings in
the auction house and chest shops, and the cosmetics and title you display. Staff can see your sanctions history. We never announce who bought or donated: no list of recent buyers, no top
buyer, no purchase announcements in game or on Discord. Of your purchases, other players see only the "Supporter"
title and its colour, shown in chat and in the player list; to hide it, write to `support@astrosky.gg` (subject:
"Store").

Other players may film or stream the game, and what you write in public chat may appear in their videos; we do not control them.

## 10. Players under 18

AstroSky is for adults. If we learn that a player is under 18 (they tell us, or a credible report shows it; a person
decides), we **remove their access, without any penalty**. This removal is not a sanction and never counts as an
offence. We explain it in simple words, and it can be contested by writing to `support@astrosky.gg`. Once any contest is
settled, we delete the data we no longer need, except data linked to a safety concern, which is kept as long as the
safety procedure requires. Purchases and donations made by a player under 18 are refunded on request. The removal is
reviewed after 12 months.

A parent or guardian can write to `support@astrosky.gg` (subject: "Privacy") with the player's username to ask us to
remove the player's access and delete their data. A person decides, as above; the player is told and can contest
before their data is deleted. We never send the parent or guardian the player's data and never ask for an identity
document.

## 12. Your rights

You can, for free:

- **see** the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- **correct** information that is wrong;
- **delete** your account data;
- get the information you gave us, and the information your play generated, in a **structured, commonly used
  format** (portability);
- **withdraw your consent** at any time by stopping to play and asking us to delete your data;
- ask about an **automated decision** (section 6).

Deletion has limits, which we explain when we answer:

- safety records cannot be deleted one by one; they are deleted automatically within 90 days;
- a sanction is kept while it applies and for 12 months after it ends;
- accounting records are kept for the legal period, without your username or UUID;
- data linked to an open incident or appeal, or to someone in danger, is kept until that ends (emails follow
  section 3), and data we must preserve by law is kept until that duty ends;
- backups keep deleted data for up to 30 more days.

**How to ask:** write to `support@astrosky.gg` with the subject "Privacy" and your Minecraft username. We will check
that the account is yours without asking for an identity document, and we will never ask for your password or a
login code. We give a player's data only to that player, and a copy never includes information about other people,
such as who reported you or their messages. We answer in writing within 30 days. If we refuse all or part of a
request, we tell you why and how to contest it.

**Complaints:** write first to the person in charge (section 1). If you disagree with our answer, you can ask the
Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca) to examine it, within 30 days of our answer, or file a
complaint with it. If you live elsewhere, you can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

## 13. How we govern privacy

- **Responsibility:** the person in charge (section 1) approves these rules and answers for them.
- **Roles:** the operator can access the server machine and its backups. One AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic), directed by the operator for operating the server only, also has restricted access to that machine. Its administrator rights are limited to a short fixed list of operations (such as checking the state of the service, stopping and restarting it, running backups, and reading the service's technical logs, which contain no player data). It reads no player data. Every command it runs is logged on the machine, and the operator can remove its access at any time. For a given case, a designated
  moderator can read the records of that case. The operator reviews appeals; a trusted adult who helps with an appeal
  can read the case under appeal. Before a suspension or a ban that follows a case where someone may be in danger,
  the operator asks, where possible, a second trusted adult to review it; that person can read the case concerned,
  on the same terms. Our moderation tools log each look at personal records, with its reason, before it happens. The other AI assistants we use to write code and documents are not allowed to connect to the server and are never given player data.
- **Retention and destruction:** each kind of data has a duration (section 3), applied by automatic deletion every
  day.
- **Complaints and requests:** handled by the person in charge, in writing, within 30 days (section 12).

## 14. How we protect it, and incidents

- The server runs on a machine rented from our host, separate from our computers.
- Access follows the roles above, and our moderation tools log every look made through them (section 4).
- Every command of the operations assistant, and whether it succeeded, is logged on the server machine.
- Backups are encrypted.

No system is perfect, and we cannot promise that an incident will never happen. We keep a register of confidentiality
incidents. If an incident creates a risk of serious harm, we notify the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec
and the people affected promptly. Since we often know players only by their username, we may also post a notice in
game, on our website and on our Discord.

## 15. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom

We use your information to provide the game you asked for, to protect players and the server (our legitimate
interest, limited by the rules above), and to meet accounting duties. In addition to the rights in section 12, you can
object to a use based on our legitimate interest, ask us to restrict a use, and complain to your data protection
authority.

## 16. Changes

apply. If we ever need to keep something longer than this policy says, we will update the policy before doing so.
Earlier versions are available on request.

This policy exists in English and in French; both versions say the same thing, and if they ever differ, the one
more favourable to you applies.
