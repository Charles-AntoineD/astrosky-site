# AstroSky — Server Rules and Terms of Play

**Version:** `2026-10-22`

**Status:** AstroSky is not open yet: the server is closed to players (whitelist) and the store has not opened. This page describes how it will work from opening day; the version date changes if anything differs.

## The short version

- AstroSky is for players **18 and over**, each with their own genuine Minecraft Java account.
- Be respectful, play fair, don't grief or scam, and don't advertise.
- Report a problem by email to `support@astrosky.gg` (subject "Report").
- You may play with more than one account, but never move coins or items from one of your accounts to another,
  directly or through an island, a shop or the auction house.
- Every sanction is decided by a person. You can appeal within 14 days.
- There are no permanent bans: a ban lasts 12 months at most, and is renewed only by a new decision based on a
  current risk.
- AstroSky is run by Charles-Antoine Daneau, not by Mojang or Microsoft.

## 1. Who can play

- You must be **18 or older**. We do not ask your age or check identity documents. If you are under 18, please do not
  join (see section 8).
- You need your own genuine Minecraft Java Edition account. Do not share your account or use someone else's.
- You must follow the [Minecraft End User License Agreement](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula) and these rules.
- AstroSky is operated by Charles-Antoine Daneau, who is responsible for the server. Mojang and Microsoft are not.
- By joining the server or buying in the store, you agree to follow these rules. Our
  [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) explains what we record and why.

## 2. Rules

### Respect

*Why: everyone should be able to play without fear.*

- No harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation.
- No hate speech or discrimination (for example about origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability).
- No sexual content, and no sexual messages to other players.
- Don't pressure anyone to share personal information or photos, to meet you, or to talk somewhere outside AstroSky.
- Never share someone's personal information (real name, address, photos, accounts elsewhere), even as a "joke".
- Don't impersonate staff or another player.
- Don't make reports you know are false. A report made in good faith is never sanctioned, even if we find nothing.

### Chat

*Why: so that chat stays readable and useful for everyone.*

- No spam or flooding, and no begging.
- No advertising for other servers, services or social accounts.
- No links to harmful or illegal sites.
- Public chat is in English or French.

### Fair play

*Why: the economy and the island ranking only mean something if everyone plays by the same rules.*

- No cheats, hacked clients or mods that give an advantage (for example x-ray, kill aura, fly, auto-clicker, macros or
  scripts that play for you).
- Mods that give no advantage are fine: performance, shaders, and interface mods that only show what the game already
  shows. For example: Sodium, Lithium, OptiFine, Iris, and minimaps without an entity radar or cave view.
- Not allowed: x-ray (resource packs included), auto-clickers, macros. Not sure about a mod? Ask us first.
- Being idle is fine, and so is being disconnected after 30 minutes idle. But don't get around that disconnection while
  you are away: no software, device, long water current or minecart loop that keeps your island loaded without you
  playing. Riding a minecart to travel is fine.
- Don't exploit bugs. Found one? Report it quickly and privately to `support@astrosky.gg`: if you do, you are not
  sanctioned.
- Never duplicate items or money.

### Islands and co-op

*Why: co-op only works if members can trust each other.*

- Don't grief, steal from or damage an island you were invited to.
- Members can use what is on your island, depending on their rank. Taking or destroying things to harm the island is
  still griefing: report it. The island owner can remove a member at any time.
- Inviting or promoting someone into your team to take what they bring (deposits, paying for an island upgrade), then
  removing them, is stealing.
- A sanction against one member never makes the other members of the island lose their progress.

### The Nether
*Why: the Nether is one world shared by everyone, inside a border; a reset keeps it fair and light.*

- The Nether is shared by all players and limited by a border. It is not protected: what you build or leave there can be broken or taken. Keep what matters on your island.
- We may reset the Nether. We announce it in game and on our Discord at least 7 days before. What is still in the Nether at the reset is lost and is not replaced.

### Trading and economy

*Why: trades should be honest, and trading for real money invites scams.*

- No scams: describe trades, auction house listings and chest shops honestly. Selling an item renamed or presented to look like
  something else is a scam.
- No real-money trading: don't buy or sell in-game items, money, islands, accounts or services for real money or for
  anything outside AstroSky. Renting out an account counts too.
- You may play with more than one account. But never move coins or items from one of your accounts to another,
  directly or through an island, a shop or the auction house, and don't use several accounts to manipulate prices.

### Accounts

*Why: a sanction must mean something, and an account belongs to one person.*

- Don't use another account to get around a sanction.
- Don't share, sell or rent accounts.

### Store and payments

*Why: buying anywhere else exposes you to scams.*

- Only buy on the official store, `astrosky.gg/store` (our Tebex store, `astrosky.tebex.store`).
- Payment fraud is against the rules. A payment dispute alone is not: we only act after a person has reviewed it and
  confirmed fraud (see the Store Terms, published when the store opens).
- If a player under 18 paid without permission, we refund and remove access without penalty (section 8); this is
  never treated as fraud.
- If anyone offers to buy you a store item, or asks for something in return for a gift, please report it. You are
  never sanctioned for being offered a gift or for reporting it.

### Staff and official channels

*Why: scammers pretend to be staff.*

- Staff will **never** ask you to give them your password, a login or verification code, your Microsoft account
  details or a payment, in private messages, by email or anywhere else. No sanction is ever lifted or shortened for
  a payment.
- Our only official addresses are listed on `astrosky.gg`.

## 3. Report

- **Report by email:** write to `support@astrosky.gg` with the subject "Report". Use email for any report, and above all for anything serious or private. Don't attach sexual images, especially if someone may be a minor: describe what you saw.
- **If someone is in immediate danger,** call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the United States) first,
  then email us with the subject "Report" to say what happened.

What happens next: a person reviews each report, within 48 hours (24 hours for serious harassment). We never name the
person who reported, although the facts can sometimes make it clear.

**What we record:** public chat and private messages are recorded for safety and deleted after 90 days at most. They
are read only for a report, an incident or a written rule; a private message only if one of its participants reports
it, for an incident or a rule about one of them, or if someone may be in danger. Before each look through our moderation
tools, its reason is logged. Nobody watches them live. Private messages between players are turned off for now; we will announce it before turning them on. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## 4. Sanctions

Every sanction is decided by a person and is proportionate to how serious the problem is and to repeat offences.

### Levels

| Level | Measure |
|---|---|
| 0 | Warning: a reminder of the rule |
| 1 | Mute |
| 2 | Restriction of a feature (chat, invitations, trading) |
| 3 | Social limitation (co-op, island visits) |
| 4 | Removal from the current session (kick) |
| 5 | Temporary suspension |
| 6 | Ban, **12 months at most** |

### Sanctions grid

| How serious (examples) | 1st time | 2nd time | 3rd time and more |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** — spam, offensive language, advertising another server | warning | 1-hour mute | 24-hour mute |
| **2** — griefing or stealing on a shared island, harassment that is not serious, exploiting a bug without duplication, impersonating staff, a report you knew was false, trading for real money | restriction of a feature or social limitation, for 24 hours | 3-day suspension | 14-day suspension |
| **3** — proven cheating (mods that give an advantage included), duplicating items or money, payment fraud confirmed by a person, serious harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, pressuring someone for photos, personal information or a meeting, sharing someone's personal information | 30-day suspension | ban of up to 12 months | — |
| **3, most serious** — sexual exploitation of a child (including grooming: gaining a child's trust in order to abuse them), sextortion, a credible threat of violence | ban of up to 12 months | — | — |

- We apply this grid. Getting around a sanction with another account gets the same measure as the sanction you got
  around. Chatting in another language than English or French only gets a reminder.
- **Scams, and moving coins or items between your own accounts,** are seriousness **2** in the grid. The first time: only a warning; after that, the grid applies from its "1st time" column.
- For a rule not listed, the person applies the closest level of this grid and names it when it tells you about the sanction.
- **Repeat offences** count as long as the previous sanction is kept: while it applies and for 12 months after it
  ends.
- **No automated sanctions:** no automated tool mutes, suspends or bans anyone, and the anti-cheat only alerts us.
  If you send public chat messages too fast (more than 5 in 10 seconds), the extra ones are not sent and you see a
  notice (in French for now). The server can also disconnect you automatically: after 30 minutes idle, or if your
  client floods the chat or sends too much data. You can rejoin right away; none of this is a sanction or counts as
  a repeat offence. If we ever add an automated measure, it will be short (1 hour at most, or removal from the
  session), at most 3 per account in 24 hours, and we will update these rules before it applies.
- **We don't announce or publish** sanctions or lists of sanctioned players.
- **Bans** are always decided by a person and last 12 months at most. When a ban ends, it is reviewed: it is lifted,
  or renewed only by a new decision with written reasons, based on a current risk. **There are no permanent bans.**
- **Someone in danger:** we handle it before anything else, outside this grid. We can block the suspected author's
  access, never that of the person in danger, for **7 days at most** while we review the case. This protective
  measure is explained to the player, unless the law requires us to wait; it can be contested and does not count as a
  repeat offence. After 7 days it is lifted, or replaced by a sanction from this grid decided by a person; it is never
  simply extended.

## 5. Appeals

- You can appeal any sanction **within 14 days** of being told about it.
- Write to `support@astrosky.gg` with the subject "Appeal" and your Minecraft username. Say which sanction you are
  appealing and why.
- We answer **within 7 days**. A suspension or a ban stays in place during the appeal.
- The operator re-examines the whole case with your reasons, and may ask another trusted adult to help. The operator
  can confirm, reduce or cancel the sanction, and tells you why.
- Only want to understand a sanction? Write to us with the subject "Sanction question". You can still appeal within
  the 14 days. We answer within 7 days. If your message asks us to change the sanction, we treat it as an appeal.

## 6. Changes to the server

AstroSky is a live game: features, prices of in-game items and balance can change. We announce important changes in
game and on our Discord. Server sell prices (`/worth`) are reviewed every week: a change applies as soon as it is
announced, never to past sales. We may stop the server; if we decide to close it for good, we will announce it in game and on our
Discord at least 30 days in advance, unless a sudden event prevents it (see the Store Terms, published when the store opens, for purchases).

## 7. Changes to these rules

We post each new version here with its date and announce important changes before they apply. A new rule never
applies to something done before it was published.

## 8. Players under 18

If we learn that a player is under 18 (a person decides), we remove their access **without any penalty**. It is not a
sanction and does not count as an offence. We explain it simply, and it can be contested by writing to
`support@astrosky.gg`. After 12 months, the removal is reviewed. Purchases and donations are refunded on request. A parent
or guardian can write to `support@astrosky.gg` (subject: "Privacy") with the player's username to ask us to remove the
player's access and delete their data. A person decides, as above; the player is told and can contest before their
data is deleted. We never send the parent or guardian the player's data and never ask for an identity document. Details are in the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
