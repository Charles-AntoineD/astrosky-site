# AstroSky — Contact

**Version:** `2026-10-19`

## Who runs AstroSky

AstroSky is operated by **Charles-Antoine Daneau**, Québec, Canada. We, not Mojang or Microsoft, are responsible for the
server, its store and its website. Purchases are sold by Tebex Limited, which handles payments (our Store Terms are
published when the store opens).

## How to reach us

Our official contact is **`support@astrosky.gg`**. AstroSky is run by one person, so please use the subject that fits your message: it helps us answer faster. We answer in English or French, and
subjects in French are welcome too.

| Subject | For | We answer |
|---|---|---|
| Support | a question or a problem in game | within 7 days |
| Report | a player who breaks the rules; anything serious or private | reviewed within 48 hours (24 hours for serious harassment) |
| Sanction question | understanding a sanction (you can still appeal within the 14 days) | within 7 days |
| Appeal | contesting a sanction (within 14 days of being told about it) | within 7 days |
| Store | a purchase, a delivery or a refund (add your Tebex transaction ID) | within 72 hours |
| Privacy | seeing, correcting or deleting your data, or any privacy question | within 30 days |
| Business | creators, press, partners | when we can |

Always include your **Minecraft username**. Never send us your password, your Microsoft account details or your card
number: we will never ask for them. Don't attach sexual images, especially if someone may be a minor: describe what
you saw.

**If someone is in immediate danger,** call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the United States) first,
then write to us with the subject "Report".

**Parents and guardians** can write to us with the subject "Privacy" and the player's username, to ask us to remove
the player's access and delete their data. A person decides; the player is told and can contest before their data
is deleted. We never send them the player's data and never ask for an identity document.

## Person in charge of the protection of personal information

The operator of AstroSky — `support@astrosky.gg` (subject: "Privacy"). Our Privacy Policy is published before the server opens to players.
