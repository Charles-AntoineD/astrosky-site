Join the AstroSky Discord

Our community space, in English and French: announcements, help with the game and the other players.

AstroSky is for players 18 and over. We never ask for your age or an ID. If you’re under 18, please don’t join the game; if we learn a member is under 18, we remove access kindly, not as a punishment.

Join the Discord

Read the rules in #rules-règles when you arrive, and the server rules for the game. Staff never ask for your password, codes or a payment.

The official way to join is this page. Any other link claiming to be AstroSky’s Discord is not ours.

To report a member, write to us: see the contact page (subject “Report”), never in a public channel. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours, not live; if someone is in danger, call emergency services first. Discord’s Report button goes to Discord, not to us.

Discord is a separate service with its own terms and privacy policy. The few things we keep from our Discord (copies of some messages that AutoMod blocks) are described in our privacy policy.
