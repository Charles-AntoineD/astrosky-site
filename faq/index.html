Questions and answers

Server address: astrosky.gg · Minecraft: Java Edition 26.2

What is AstroSky?

A co-op OneBlock SkyBlock server for Minecraft: Java Edition: you start on a single block floating among the stars and grow it into an island with your friends. AstroSky is run by an independent operator, not by Mojang or Microsoft.

What is the server address and version?

astrosky.gg, on Minecraft: Java Edition 26.2. Step by step: how to join.

Can I play on Bedrock, a console or a phone?

No. AstroSky is for Minecraft: Java Edition on a computer only.

When does AstroSky open?

Opens Monday, November 16, 2026, at 6 p.m. Eastern Time (Québec time). Until then, the server is closed to players (whitelist).

Is it free?

Yes. You need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition; the rest is free.

Who can play?

Players 18 and over. We never ask for your age or an ID. If you’re under 18, please don’t join; if we learn a player is under 18, we remove access kindly, not as a punishment. A parent or guardian can write to us from the contact page (subject “Privacy”) to ask us to remove a player’s access and delete their data. A person reviews each request; the player is told and can contest it.

Can I play with friends?

Yes: invite them to your island and build it together; your team shares challenges and the island bank.

Can you buy an advantage?

No. The store sells cosmetics and donations only, such as titles and name colours. Nothing sold changes how the game plays, coins are never sold, and no purchase is random.

What are the rules?

Read them before you play: AstroSky rules (sanctions and appeals included).

How do I report a player or a problem?

Write to us from the contact page with the subject “Report”: we review reports within 48 hours (24 hours for serious harassment). If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the United States) first.

Is there a Discord?

Not yet. When it opens, its invite will be posted only on this site.

What’s next?

Update 1 is planned: jobs and bigger island teams.

How do I contact you?

From the contact page. We answer in English or French. Staff never ask for your password, codes or a payment.
